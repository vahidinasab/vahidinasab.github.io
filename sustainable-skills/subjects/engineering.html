Engineering GCSE and clean energy jobs

4 GCSE topics to look out for, and the 8 clean energy jobs that use them.

From the Department for Education's subject content for each GCSE in England. Exam boards add detail, so your course may cover more. Electronics and Engineering are not offered at every school.

GCSE topics to look out for

  • Programmable control systems

    The control systems that keep wind turbines and heat pumps running.

    What the course says (official wording)“how to design and construct programs which monitor and control systems with the use of inputs, processes and outputs within an engineered system”

    Used by Wind turbine technician Heat pump engineer Electronics engineer Building services engineer Engineering maintenance technician

  • Energy, maintenance and end of life

    Planning the servicing of a wind farm, and what happens to old panels and batteries.

    What the course says (official wording)“design solutions need to consider energy production methods and engineered lifespans including planned obsolescence, the need for maintenance of machinery and end of life (EOL)”

    Used by Wind turbine technician Renewable energy engineer Solar panel installer Engineering maintenance technician

  • Testing under load

    Making sure turbine blades and roof fixings are strong enough.

    What the course says (official wording)“the methods of testing and evaluating materials, and structural behaviour under load”

    Used by Wind turbine technician Solar panel installer Engineering maintenance technician

  • Engineering and the environment

    Weighing up the environmental gains and costs of an energy project.

    What the course says (official wording)“the impact of engineering on the environment and sustainable development”

    Used by Renewable energy engineer Sustainability practitioner

Source: Engineering GCSE subject content (Department for Education)
