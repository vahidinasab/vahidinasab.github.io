Design and technology GCSE and clean energy jobs

5 GCSE topics to look out for, and the 8 clean energy jobs that use them.

From the Department for Education's subject content for each GCSE in England. Exam boards add detail, so your course may cover more.

GCSE topics to look out for

  • Energy generation and storage

    Choosing a solar panel and battery to power a product.

    What the course says (official wording)“how energy is generated and stored in order to choose and use appropriate sources to make products and to power systems”

    Used by Solar panel installer Electronics engineer Renewable energy engineer

  • Electronic systems and smart controls

    The sensors and controls in smart heating and smart chargers.

    What the course says (official wording)“how electronic systems provide functionality to products and processes, including sensors and control devices to respond to a variety of inputs, and devices to produce a range of outputs”

    Used by Electronics engineer Smart home installer Heat pump engineer Electrician Building services engineer

  • Programmable components

    The programmable parts inside smart thermostats, plugs, meters and chargers.

    What the course says (official wording)“the use of programmable components to embed functionality into products in order to enhance and customise their operation”

    Used by Smart home installer Electronics engineer Building services engineer

  • Life cycle assessment

    Picking materials with a smaller footprint, from mining to recycling.

    What the course says (official wording)“the basic principles in carrying out a life-cycle assessment of a material or product”

    Used by Sustainability practitioner Electronics engineer

  • Your design project

    Half of your marks come from designing and making a prototype. Exam boards set new contexts each year, and where one allows an energy theme you could design something like a solar-powered light.

    What the course says (official wording)“securing a sustainable future”

    The quotation is one of the contexts the Department for Education suggests for the project.

    Used by Solar panel installer Electronics engineer Renewable energy engineer

Source: Design and technology GCSE subject content (Department for Education)

Around the home

  • Solar panels: Energy generation and storage
  • Loft and wall insulation: Life cycle assessment
  • Smart meter and smart home controls: Programmable components
