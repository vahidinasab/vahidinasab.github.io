Electrical engineering technician

Fits, tests and fixes electrical equipment and control systems, including in wind turbines and heat pumps.

  • Installs
  • Maintains and repairs

Pay

£24,000 to £48,000 a year

From starting pay to experienced. This is the typical yearly pay on the job's official profile.

A day might include

Testing a control panel, repairing a motor, and checking the electrics inside a wind turbine or heat pump.

Skills you use

  • Electrical testing
  • Reading diagrams
  • Problem solving

It could suit you if you enjoy

  • Gadgets, electronics and coding
  • Making and fixing things
  • Science and how things work

GCSE topics it uses

From the Department for Education's subject content for each GCSE in England. Exam boards add detail, so your course may cover more.

Science

  • The National Grid
    How power gets from a wind farm to your street, and why substations are needed.
  • Mains electricity and safety
    Wiring a home charger, solar system or heat pump so that it is safe.
  • Batteries and the mains
    Home batteries and solar panels work with direct current while the mains is alternating current, so an inverter converts between the two.
  • Electric motors
    How the motor in an electric car turns the wheels.

Electronics

  • Switching circuits
    Switching power on and off safely, which chargers and solar inverters do all the time.

Ways in

  • Apprenticeship: Engineering Technician (level 3)
  • Apprenticeship: Power Industry Substation Fitter (level 3)
  • T Level: Engineering, Manufacturing, Processing and Control
What do these words mean?
Apprenticeship
A real job with training. You are paid, learn at work and study part of the time, often at a college. You can start from age 16.
T Level
A two-year course after GCSEs, worth the same as three A levels. It includes at least 45 days working with an employer.
Levels 2 to 7
Level 2 is the same standard as GCSEs at grades 4 to 9. Level 3 is the same as A levels and T Levels. Levels 4 and 5 are between A levels and a degree. Level 6 is a bachelor's degree, and level 7 a master's degree.
