Privacy

How this tool handles your information

Last updated 10 November 2026

The short version

  • Your answers stay on your device. We never see them.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address or school.
  • There are no accounts, no adverts and no cookies.
  • We count visits so we know how many people use the tool. The count does not include your answers or who you are.
  • If you email or save your roadmap, you decide where it goes.

The details, for parents, carers and teachers

Who runs this tool

The Sustainable Local Energy Skills Roadmap is a free guide made by Professor Vahid Vahidinasab at the Centre for Sustainable Innovation, University of Salford. You can read how the University handles personal data on its data protection, privacy and cookies page.

Your answers

Your answers to the four questions are saved in your browser's own storage on your device, so they are still there when you come back. They are never sent to us or anyone else. To delete them, press Start again, or clear this website's data in your browser settings. If you use a shared computer, press Start again when you finish.

The visit counter

When the tool is opened on its website, your browser sends a short, anonymous message to a counter that runs on Cloudflare's network. The message only says what happened, such as "a visit", "a roadmap was made" or "a PDF was downloaded". Cloudflare uses the connection to work out the country and, in the UK, the region the visit came from. The counter keeps only running totals by country and region. It does not keep IP addresses, your answers or anything that could identify you, and it does not use cookies.

Like any website, the services that deliver the page (GitHub, which hosts it, and Cloudflare, which runs the counter) may briefly process technical details such as your IP address to send the page and keep their services secure. See GitHub's privacy statement and Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Fonts and the PDF maker

The fonts and the code that makes your PDF are stored on this website, so opening the tool does not send requests to font services or other outside services. Your PDF is made inside your browser and saved straight to your device.

Emailing or copying your roadmap

If you choose Email it, the tool opens Gmail, Outlook or your email app with a message ready to send. That email service's own privacy terms apply, and this tool cannot see the email. Copy text only copies your roadmap to your device's clipboard.

Links to other websites

The tool links to official websites such as GOV.UK and the official careers services. They have their own privacy notices.

Children

The tool is made for pupils aged 11 to 16 and their families. It does not ask for personal details, and it has no accounts, chat or messaging.

Changes and questions

If the way the tool handles information changes, this page will be updated first. If you have a question about privacy, contact Professor Vahid Vahidinasab through his University of Salford staff profile, or use Send feedback on the roadmap page.
