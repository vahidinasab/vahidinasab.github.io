### Project descriptions

**Horizon Europe DriVe2X.** Delivering Renewal and Innovation to mass Vehicle Electrification enabled by V2X technologies. Led the development of the concept, the formation of the consortium and the writing of the proposal from a standing start. The programme develops knowledge, tools, models and technologies for mass electric vehicle deployment enabled by V2X (vehicle-to-home, vehicle-to-building, vehicle-to-grid), including customer-centric local flexibility markets, analysis of grid impacts, and real-world pilot demonstration.

The grant was secured while at Nottingham Trent University and delivered there for two years. **It transferred to the University of Salford on 1 January 2025**, where the remaining two and a half years of delivery sit, and where the majority of the technical development and the new ideas within the programme have been shaped.

**EU H2020 inteGRIDy.** One of the largest smart distribution grid projects funded by the EU, with a consortium of 30 companies and universities across the UK and Europe. Served as Project Manager and Research Associate, delivering the Integrated Simulation Environment, a multi-level analysis tool enabling scenario-based assessment of distribution networks to support network smartening. The tool supports decision-makers in selecting planning and operation strategies and in analysing future development scenarios. Included the Isle of Wight pilot, with direct engagement of local stakeholders and the public.

**EPSRC Active Building Centre Research Programme.** Research and development in the control and optimisation of distributed energy systems, including renewable sources, storage and electric vehicles, at building and distribution network level. Focused on optimising the profitability of energy-positive buildings through coordinated energy scheduling while meeting internal comfort requirements, and on the provision of flexibility and demand-side response to the distribution grid.

**EPSRC National Centre for Energy Systems Integration.** Collaborative research developing a vector-coupling definition of storage based on Power-to-Gas technology, distinguishing Single-Vector Storage from Vector-Coupling Storage, and demonstrating the flexibility value of storage that charges from one energy vector and discharges into another, thereby connecting the gas and electricity networks.

**EPSRC Supergen Energy Networks Hub.** Investigation of the value of Power-to-Gas as a vector-coupling technology in heavily constrained electricity networks, particularly under conditions of high renewable generation and low demand, demonstrating how a holistic whole-system approach enhances efficiency while reducing costs.

**Scenario Development of Lines and Substations Manoeuvre.** Developed an optimal structure for distribution line and substation manoeuvre in the Qazvin electric distribution network accounting for reliability and load uncertainty. Delivered as a software platform, which remains in active use by the distribution network operator.

**Environmental Techno-Economic Assessment of Microgrids.** A National Elite Foundation fellowship that established the foundation of the smart distribution grid and microgrid research line, and led to the founding of the Soha Smart Energy Systems Laboratory.

**Development of Optimal Bidding Strategy of Generation Companies.** A hybrid scheduling model with an advanced forecasting engine to analyse electricity market trends and derive optimal operation strategies. The resulting platform operates in Tehran, Gilan and a number of other Iranian generation companies.

**Energy Management for Energy Intensive Industries of Iran.** An international project with UNIDO and the Iranian Fuel Conservation Company that produced high-level policy and guidelines for energy management across the seven most energy-intensive industries in Iran.

**Electricity Price Forecasting.** A hybrid artificial neural network and fuzzy price forecasting method fitted to the specific regulations of the Iran electricity market. Became the core forecasting tool for the subsequent optimal bidding strategy work, and remains in use in a number of companies.

**National and Local Electric Load Forecasting.** A hybrid forecast engine for short, medium and long-term electric load prediction using AI and machine learning techniques. Recognised at the Annual Research Festival of the Ministry of Energy. The software is in use at the National Grid ESO of Iran and at several regional electricity companies including Khoozestan and Bakhtar.

## 7. Research interests and areas of activity

The net-zero transition rests on three foundations: green generation; storage systems and demand-side potential; and electrification. Current research centres on **local energy communities for net zero**, seeking to enhance system resilience and empower people through a whole-system approach.

**Three research pillars:**
- Smart homes, microgrids and grids
- Resilience and flexibility in local energy communities
- Empowering people and communities in the net-zero-carbon transition

**Technical areas:** smart grids and microgrids; electricity market design and restructured power systems; peer-to-peer energy trading and local transactive energy markets; local flexibility markets; electric vehicle integration and V2G/V2X; whole-energy and multi-vector systems spanning power, gas, heat and transport; Power-to-Gas and green hydrogen; energy storage, including battery degradation and lifetime extension; active buildings and demand-side response; distribution network resilience, reliability and expansion planning; artificial intelligence, machine learning and advanced optimisation for energy forecasting and decision-making under uncertainty.
